06 · Finding #2

The larger gap was further upstream

The funnel was decomposed again to ask whether visitors were even reaching individual product pages.

60%

did not reach a Product Detail Page

900 of 1,500 sessions had no recorded Product View.

What “Product View” means

Product View means entering a Product Detail Page (PDP). Seeing a product card on the homepage or collection page does not count.

The funnel could locate where progression stopped, but still could not explain why.

09 · Business decision

What should the owner do next?

The evidence is strong enough to prioritize the next area of work, but not strong enough to claim which UI, merchandising, or pricing factor caused the behavior.

Priority: Focus on Collection → Product engagement before spending more resources on checkout optimization or simply acquiring more traffic.

1 · Measure first

Instrument Product Card Impression → Product Card Click → PDP.

2 · Test hypotheses

Product imagery, price / promotion visibility, card information, product ordering, and mobile interaction remain hypotheses—not conclusions.

3 · Run controlled experiments

Change one meaningful element at a time and measure whether Collection → PDP progression improves. Formal A/B testing may be limited by traffic volume.

What the evidence does NOT prove

It does not prove that product images are bad, prices are too high, the CTA is unclear, assortment is wrong, or mobile UX caused the problem.

The analysis identified where to focus the next investigation—not a causal explanation the data could not support.

10 · AI & analytical ownership

AI accelerated the work. It did not own the conclusion.

Because ecommerce was a new domain, AI accelerated domain exploration, SQL drafting, and hypothesis generation. Analytical judgment centered on metric denominators, source/grain decisions, benchmark comparability, evidence thresholds, interpretation, and when to reject unsupported explanations.

Analytics is not only about finding an answer. Sometimes its value is narrowing uncertainty enough to decide what should be measured, tested, or changed next.
